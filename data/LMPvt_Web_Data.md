**Why chose us:**

Logmate Engineering & Services Pvt. Ltd. stands as a trusted engineering partner with over 22 years of experience in delivering reliable and performance-driven power solutions across Pakistan. Our strength lies not only in supplying equipment but in engineering complete, integrated power systems tailored to the operational needs of industrial, commercial, and institutional clients. From diesel and gas gensets to large-scale solar installations, transformers, and custom-built HT/LT panels under our in-house brand LOGMAK, we offer turnkey EPC solutions backed by technical expertise and practical field experience.

**Our Businesses:**

**Genset:**

Logmate Engineering & Services Pvt. Ltd. operates as a comprehensive power and electro-mechanical engineering company offering diversified solutions under one integrated platform. Our core expertise begins with diesel and gas genset solutions, where we provide sales, purchase, rental, and leasing services for industrial and commercial clients. We undertake complete supply, installation, commissioning, synchronization, load management systems, and long-term maintenance contracts to ensure operational continuity and efficiency.

**Solar:**

In the renewable energy sector, our Solar Energy Division delivers complete solar power solutions including on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid systems. We execute projects under various financial models such as outright sale, rental, leasing, BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer), and PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) structures. From system design and engineering to procurement, installation, net metering approvals, and long-term operation & maintenance, Logmate provides end-to-end solar EPC services tailored to industrial and corporate energy demands.

**Transformer:**

Our transformer division specializes in both WAPDA and Non-WAPDA transformers, including supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and upgradation services. We facilitate regulatory approvals and ensure compliance with national standards, delivering reliable distribution and power transformers suitable for industrial and commercial operations.

**HT/LT Panels:**

Under our in-house manufacturing brand LOGMAK, we design, fabricate, assemble, and commission high-quality HT and LT panels. Our product range includes PCC panels, MCC panels, ATS panels, synchronization panels, capacitor banks, and customized industrial control panels, engineered according to international standards and tested for durability, safety, and performance.

**Elevator:**

Additionally, through our joint venture partnership with Emfore Corporation, Logmate provides advanced elevator and escalator solutions for commercial buildings, malls, industrial facilities, and corporate projects. We handle complete supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance services to ensure safety, reliability, and modern vertical transportation systems.

**Electric Equipment supply:**

Beyond these core domains, Logmate also supplies a wide range of electrical equipment including HT/LT cables, breakers, switchgear, busbars, and associated accessories, supporting clients with complete electrical infrastructure solutions under one trusted engineering partner.
