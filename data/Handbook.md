#### ABOUT COMPANY

In the 21st century, generator companies continue to innovate and adapt to changing market dynamics and customer needs. The focus has shifted towards sustainability, with companies developing more efficient and environmentally-friendly generator solutions. Additionally, advancements in digital technology have led to the development of smart generators with remote monitoring and control capabilities **Logmate Engineering & Services** established in 2004 and it is a dynamic and customer-centric provider of generator sets (gensets) and comprehensive power solutions. With a steadfast commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we cater to a diverse clientele across various industries, ensuring reliable and efficient power supply for their critical operations. Due to the diversified need of the services we introduced a sister concerns with the names of **M/s XG Solutions** which own a large number of diesel generators and equipment to fulfill the prevailing demand of the telecom, oil and gas, mining and construction sectors of the country.
